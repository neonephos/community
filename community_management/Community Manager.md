# NeoNephos Community Manager

## 1. Purpose of the Role
The NeoNephos Community Manager ensures alignment, transparency, and collaboration across the NeoNephos ecosystem. The role connects technical teams, leadership bodies, project-level community managers, and the broader open-source community. It combines **communication governance**, **project coordination** and **community engagement** to maintain a healthy and productive ecosystem.

## 2. Role Objectives

### Strategic Objectives
- Ensure clear and consistent communication across all NeoNephos projects and committees.
- Provide clear and comprehensive visibility into project status, associated risks, and overall progress.
- Foster strong engagement and build sustained trust within the community.
- Ensure full adherence to open‑source processes, the Code of Conduct, and all publication guidelines.
- Enable efficient decision‑making through well‑prepared meetings and a structured flow of information.
  
### Operational Objectives
- Maintain consistent sync between project CMs, Outreach and TSC.
- Support standardized, timely, and consistent updates and reports across all projects
- Ensure the efficient handling of inbound requests, including publications, due‑diligence activities, and community inquiries.

## 3. Cross-Functional Collaboration
The Community Manager acts as a connective layer who fosters alignment, communication, and collaboration across all organizational groups and stakeholders. 

Those groups are:

### Governance and Executive Oversight Team
- **Governing Board** – strategic alignment, community visibility, escalations  
- **Technical Steering Committee (TSC)** – roadmap alignment, cross-project impacts  
- **Technical Advisory Committee (TAC)** – technical standards, architectural coherence  

### Execution Teams
- **Project Community Managers (CMs)** – project status, needs, updates  
- **Design / Development Teams** – collaborate closely to gain early insights into upcoming changes and coordinate activities together.
- **Outreach Committee** – communication, social media, ecosystem engagement  
- **OSPO & Legal** – open-source compliance and publication processes  

### External Community Network
- Independent experts and external contributors 
- Partner organizations  
- Representatives from relevant open‑source foundations  

## 4. Overview of the Community Manager’s Steering and Meeting Responsibilities

### 4.1 Participation in Organizational Committees

| Meeting | Purpose | Community Manager Contribution |
|--------|----------|--------------------------------|
| **Project Internal Meetings** | Track technical and project progress | Gather insights, identify dependencies, surface risks |
| **TAC** | Standards & architecture | Represent community needs |
| **TSC** | Technical direction | Sync with project community managers |
| **Governing Board** | Strategic decisions | Communicate community concerns, requirements, and achievements at a strategic level | 
| **Town Halls** | Share major updates | Share highlights, gather feedback |

### 4.2 Meetings the CM Organizes or Moderates

| Meeting | Audience | Role |
|--------|-----------|------|
| **Community Call (public)** | All community members | Moderate, present updates, foster communication |
| **NeoNephos-internal Meeting** | Internal stakeholders | Prepare the agenda for the external community call and ensure cross-project alignment |


## 5. Responsibilities & Deliverables

### 5.1 Project Coordination & Monitoring
- Regularly request status updates and activity plans from project community managers (CMs).
- Compile agendas for CM calls based on the collected information.
- Consolidate submitted project insights to support consistent reporting.
  
### 5.2 Communication & Community Engagement
- Supervise community channels to facilitate constructive discussions and maintain a healthy communication environment
- Oversee a unified communication calendar and help to determine the scheduling of announcements and content releases.
- Partner with teams and contributors to bring high‑quality, community‑oriented content to blogs, social media, milestones, and podcasts.
- Promote awareness of ethical communication standards while reporting any deviations from the Code of Conduct.
  
### 5.3 Handling Outbound Requests
- Guide teams through publication workflows  
- Support internal and external due diligence  
- Provide “publication readiness” checks 


## 6. KPIs & Success Metrics

### Engagement Metrics
- Participation in community calls and meetings
- Growth of active contributors and members
- Activity across communication channels (messages, active users)
- Number of successful publications and social media activities  

# Summary
Community management is a vital function within NeoNephos, connecting different departments and projects while ensuring clear communication and coordinated execution. Through continuous engagement with the Governing Board, Design, Development, Outreach Committee, Governance, and the broader community, the Community Manager ensures alignment, transparency, and progress toward shared goals.
