# Position Paper: Community Manager for NeoNephos

## Introduction

Community management plays a central role in coordinating communication and collaboration within and beyond the organization. It ensures effective alignment between departments and projects, and supports an active, informed, and engaged community.  
This document outlines the responsibilities of the NeoNephos Community Manager and describes the interfaces with other organizational bodies.

---

## Relevant Meetings for a NeoNephos Community Manager

### Participation

#### **Project-Internal Meetings**
- **Purpose**: Track project-specific developments and decisions.  
- **Role**: Stay informed about agendas, milestones, and challenges.

#### **Technical Advisory Committee (TAC)**
- **Purpose**: Coordinate technical standards and architectural decisions.  
- **Role**: Represent community-related topics and ensure they align with technical standards.

#### **Technical Steering Committee (TSC)**
- **Purpose**: Guide the technical direction and oversee development progress.  
- **Role**: Align community activities with the technical roadmap and ensure mutual awareness.

#### **Governing Board**
- **Purpose**: Define the strategic direction and governance of NeoNephos.  
- **Role**: Communicate community concerns, requirements, and achievements at a strategic level.

#### **Town Hall Meetings**
- **Purpose**: Share progress updates and discuss major project developments.  
- **Role**: Participate actively to remain informed and contribute when relevant.

---

### Organization and Moderation

#### **NeoNephos-Internal Meeting**
- **Purpose**: Coordinate project dependencies and share updates among internal stakeholders.  
- **Role**: Prepare the agenda for the external community call and ensure cross-project alignment.

#### **Community Call**
- **Purpose**: Open forum for all interested parties to present milestones, achievements, and upcoming plans.  
- **Role**: Moderate discussions, present updates, and ensure productive, transparent communication.

---

### Summary

The Community Manager participates in key meetings to remain aligned with ongoing developments, organizes community-oriented events, and ensures consistent communication across committees, working groups, and leadership bodies.

---

## Tasks of the NeoNephos Community Manager

### 1. Project Coordination and Status Monitoring
- Regularly request status updates and activity plans from project community managers (CMs).
- Compile agendas for CM calls based on the collected information.
- Gather relevant analytics across all projects to support consistent reporting.

### 2. Communication and Requirements Management
- Maintain continuous communication with project CMs regarding needs from OSPO, NeoNephos, or Outreach.
- Monitor and moderate relevant communication channels (e.g., Zulip, Teams).
- Evaluate the existing set of communication tools and explore consolidation options into a unified platform.
- Curate project news for distribution across social media, websites, blogs, and podcasts.
- Support the publication process of research papers.
- Enforce ethical communication standards and adherence to the Code of Conduct.
- Collect and document community requirements related to processes and documentation.

### 3. Organization of Meetings
- Plan and facilitate internal (SAP-only), NeoNephos-wide, and external community meetings.

### 4. Handling Outbound Requests from NeoNephos Projects
- Provide guidance on open-source publication processes.
- Support due diligence activities (SAP and LFEU/NeoNephos).
- Oversee or support project publication activities.

---

## Summary

Community management is a vital function within NeoNephos, connecting different departments and projects while ensuring clear communication and coordinated execution. Through continuous engagement with the Governing Board, Design, Development, Outreach Committee, Governance, and the broader community, the Community Manager ensures alignment, transparency, and progress toward shared goals.
